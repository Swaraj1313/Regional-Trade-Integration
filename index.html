Trade & Regional Integration Networks
An ongoing research programme examining structural patterns in global trade through network analysis, gravity modelling, and trade intensity indicators. Each module explores regional connectivity, unrealised trade potential, and structural integration depth across Asia and Africa.

AfCFTA  ·  Phase 2  ·  Gravity Model
AfCFTA Corridor Diagnostic Framework
Gravity-model based diagnostic of African trade corridors identifying unrealised trade potential, structural bottlenecks, inclusion gaps, and the probable constraint type for each bilateral pair. Classifies 2,862 corridors across 54 countries into three intervention categories aligned with distinct development finance objectives.
